# Uploads

There is three ways to upload content on your Funkwhale {term}`pod`.

- Upload music you own but you don't make
- Upload music you make : this will create an artist channel
- Upload podcast : this will create an podcast channel

## Music you own

You can set the privacy level for each upload on the Manage Upload tab on your profile.

- **Everyone, across all instances** – the upload is available to everybody who interacts with your pod. This includes users of other pods.
- **Everyone on this instance** – the upload is available to all users on your pod. This does not include users of other pods.
- **Followers** - the upload is available to your followers.
- **Nobody except me** – the upload is only available to you and people you share the library with.

## Channels

If you upload content to channels, the content will be public. This applies for music you make and podcasts.
